# Service Level Agreement (SUPERSEDED)

**Northwind Cloud** · Version 1.0 · In force 1 June 2020 to 31 December 2024

> **No longer in force.** Replaced by version 2.0 (DOC-SLA-001) on
> 1 January 2025. Retained because tickets opened during this period are
> assessed against these targets, not the current ones.

## 1. First-response targets

| Priority | Enterprise | Professional | Starter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **30 minutes** | 60 minutes | 4 hours |
| **P2** | 60 minutes | 2 hours | 8 hours |
| **P3** | 4 hours | 8 hours | 16 hours |
| **P4** | 8 hours | 16 hours | 24 hours |

## 2. Resolution targets

| Priority | Enterprise | Professional | Starter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | 4 hours | 8 hours | 24 hours |
| **P2** | 8 hours | 16 hours | 48 hours |
| **P3** | 48 hours | 72 hours | 120 hours |
| **P4** | 120 hours | 168 hours | 240 hours |

| Tier | Uptime target |
|---|---|
| Enterprise | 99.90 percent |
| Professional | 99.50 percent |
| Starter | 99.00 percent |
