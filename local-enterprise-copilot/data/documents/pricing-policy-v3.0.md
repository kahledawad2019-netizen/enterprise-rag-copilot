# Pricing and Discounting Policy

**Northwind Cloud** · Version 3.0 · Effective 1 July 2025

Access group: **finance**. This document contains commercially sensitive
discounting thresholds and is not shared with customers.

## 1. List pricing

1.1 All published prices are per month, in USD, exclusive of tax. EMEA
customers are billed in EUR at the rate published quarterly.

1.2 Annual contracts are billed at a reduced monthly rate, typically 17 percent
below the monthly-plan rate. The discount is built into the plan price and is
not a negotiated discount for the purposes of section 2.

## 2. Discount authority

2.1 **The maximum discount that may be applied to any subscription is 25
percent of list price.** This is a hard ceiling. No sales representative,
manager or director may exceed it.

2.2 Approval thresholds within the ceiling:

| Discount | Approver |
|---|---|
| 0 - 10 percent | Account Executive |
| 11 - 20 percent | Sales Director |
| 21 - 25 percent | CFO |
| Above 25 percent | **Not permitted** |

2.3 Any request above 25 percent must be restructured as a shorter term, a
reduced seat count, or a phased ramp. It must not be approved as a discount.

## 3. Multi-year contracts

3.1 Two-year contracts may receive an additional 5 percent, counted **within**
the 25 percent ceiling, not in addition to it.

3.2 Three-year contracts require CFO approval regardless of discount level.

## 4. Price increases

4.1 List prices may increase at renewal. Customers receive at least 60 days
notice.

4.2 Increases at renewal are capped at 7 percent per year for customers on
Enterprise plans with continuous service.

## 5. Seat-based billing

5.1 Seats are counted as named users with access in the billing period, not
concurrent users.

5.2 Seats added mid-term are pro-rated to the end of the current term. Seats
removed mid-term do not reduce the current term's invoice; the reduction
applies from the next renewal.
