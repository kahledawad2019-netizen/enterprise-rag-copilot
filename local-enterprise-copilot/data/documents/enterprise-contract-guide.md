# Enterprise Contract Guide

**Northwind Cloud** - Version 2.2 - Effective 1 August 2025

Access group: **finance**. Internal guidance for deal desk and legal review.

## 1. Standard terms

1.1 The default Enterprise term is 12 months, commencing on the service start
date rather than the signature date.

1.2 Auto-renewal is the default. The customer may disable it with **60 days
written notice** before the renewal date.

1.3 Notice given fewer than 60 days before renewal takes effect at the end of
the following term, not the current one.

## 2. Payment terms

2.1 Standard payment terms are net 30 from the invoice date.

2.2 Net 45 and net 60 require Finance approval and are granted only for
customers with an unblemished payment history of at least 12 months.

2.3 Late payment attracts interest at 1.5 percent per month, though in practice
this is waived for customers who settle within 15 days of a reminder.

## 3. Termination

3.1 Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice
and an opportunity to cure.

3.2 Termination for convenience by the customer does not create a refund
entitlement beyond the terms of the Refund and Credit Policy.

3.3 On termination, the customer may export their data for 90 days. After that
the data is deleted per the Security and Privacy Policy.

## 4. Liability

4.1 Aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months preceding
the claim.

4.2 The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or to data-protection
obligations.

## 5. Custom SLAs

5.1 Custom SLA terms are available on Enterprise contracts above 100,000 USD
annual value, and require VP Customer Support sign-off.

5.2 A custom SLA must never commit to a first-response target below 10 minutes,
because the on-call acknowledgement standard itself is 10 minutes and no margin
would remain.

## 6. Data processing

6.1 A Data Processing Addendum is mandatory for all EMEA customers.

6.2 Sub-processor changes require 30 days notice to the customer, who may object
on reasonable data-protection grounds.
