# Customer Onboarding Guide

**Northwind Cloud** - Version 3.0 - Effective 1 October 2025

Authority level: **guidance**. Recommended practice, not a contractual
commitment. Nothing here creates an obligation that is not in the contract.

## 1. Onboarding phases

| Phase | Duration | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Kickoff | Week 1 | Success criteria agreed, stakeholders identified |
| Technical setup | Weeks 1-2 | SSO configured, data sources connected |
| Configuration | Weeks 2-4 | Dashboards or pipelines built for the first use case |
| Enablement | Weeks 4-6 | Admin and end-user training delivered |
| Handover | Week 6 | Transition to steady-state support |

## 2. Kickoff

2.1 Hold the kickoff within 5 business days of contract signature.

2.2 Agree the first use case explicitly. An onboarding without a named first
use case is the strongest single predictor of low adoption.

2.3 Identify an executive sponsor. Accounts without one churn at roughly twice
the rate.

## 3. Technical setup

3.1 SSO should be configured before any end user is invited. Retrofitting SSO
after users exist causes duplicate accounts and inflated seat counts.

3.2 Connect at least two data sources during setup. Single-source deployments
rarely expand.

## 4. Adoption targets

4.1 By day 30, at least 40 percent of purchased seats should be active.

4.2 By day 90, at least 60 percent.

4.3 An account below 20 percent seat adoption at day 60 is flagged to Customer
Success as an early churn risk, regardless of the health score.

## 5. Handover to support

5.1 Confirm that the customer knows how to raise a ticket and what their
priority definitions mean.

5.2 Confirm the SLA tier the customer is entitled to. Most escalation disputes
arise from a customer believing they have a higher tier than they purchased.
