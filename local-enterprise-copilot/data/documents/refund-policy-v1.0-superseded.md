# Refund Policy (SUPERSEDED)

**Northwind Cloud** · Version 1.0 · In force 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2024

> **This version is no longer in force.** It was replaced by the Refund and
> Credit Policy version 2.1 (DOC-REF-001) on 1 January 2025. It is retained
> only so that contracts signed during its period can be interpreted, and for
> restating historical figures.

## 1. Monthly plans

1.1 Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of
the billing period. No partial refunds.

## 2. Annual plans

2.1 **Annual contracts are final and non-refundable once signed.** No pro-rata
refund is available at any point in the term, including within the first 30
days.

2.2 Customers who wish to stop service must allow the term to run to its end
date and disable auto-renewal.

## 3. Billing errors

3.1 Confirmed billing errors are corrected by account credit, applied to the
following invoice. Cash refunds are not issued.

## 4. Approval

All refunds require VP Finance approval regardless of amount.
