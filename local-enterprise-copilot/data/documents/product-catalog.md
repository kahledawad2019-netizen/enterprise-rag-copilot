# Product Catalog

**Northwind Cloud** · Version 4.2 · Effective 15 January 2026

## Northwind Analytics (NW-ANALYTICS)

Self-service business intelligence and dashboards for operational teams.
Launched March 2021.

| Tier | Seats included | Key capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 5 | Dashboards, 10 data sources, daily refresh |
| Professional | 25 | Scheduled reports, 50 sources, hourly refresh, API access |
| Enterprise | 100 | Unlimited sources, real-time refresh, SSO, audit log, custom SLA |

## Northwind DataPipeline (NW-PIPELINE)

Managed ETL and streaming ingestion with more than 200 connectors.
Launched September 2021.

| Tier | Seats included | Key capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 3 | 10 pipelines, daily schedule |
| Professional | 15 | 100 pipelines, streaming, transformation library |
| Enterprise | 60 | Unlimited pipelines, private networking, dedicated capacity |

## Northwind ServiceDesk (NW-DESK)

Ticketing, SLA management and support workflows. Launched June 2020, the
company's first product.

| Tier | Seats included | Key capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 10 | Ticketing, email channel |
| Professional | 40 | SLA rules, portal and chat, automation |
| Enterprise | 150 | Multi-brand, advanced routing, custom SLA policies |

## Northwind Shield (NW-SHIELD)

Audit logging, access governance and compliance reporting.
Launched January 2022. Professional and Enterprise tiers only.

| Tier | Seats included | Key capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | 20 | Audit log, access reviews, SOC 2 reporting |
| Enterprise | 80 | Continuous compliance, custom frameworks, data residency |

## Northwind Connect (NW-CONNECT)

Embedded messaging and customer notification platform.
Launched November 2022, the newest product.

| Tier | Seats included | Key capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 5 | Email and in-app notifications |
| Professional | 25 | SMS, templates, delivery analytics |
| Enterprise | 75 | Multi-region delivery, custom domains, guaranteed throughput |

## Billing intervals

Every plan is available monthly. Annual billing is available on all
Professional and Enterprise plans, and on Analytics Starter. Annual plans are
priced roughly 17 percent below the equivalent monthly rate.
