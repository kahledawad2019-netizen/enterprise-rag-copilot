# Sales Compensation and Discount Guidance

**Northwind Cloud** · Version 1.4 · Effective 1 March 2025

Authority level: **guidance**. Where this document conflicts with the Pricing
and Discounting Policy (DOC-PRC-001, authority: policy), the policy prevails.

## 1. Commission structure

1.1 Base commission is 8 percent of first-year contract value.

1.2 Commission is reduced proportionally by the discount applied:

| Discount applied | Commission rate |
|---|---|
| 0 - 10 percent | 8 percent |
| 11 - 20 percent | 6 percent |
| 21 percent and above | 4 percent |

## 2. Strategic accounts

2.1 A strategic account is one with an expected three-year value above
500,000 USD, or a recognised brand in a target vertical.

2.2 **For strategic accounts, discounts of up to 30 percent have historically
been approved** where the account opens a new vertical or provides a public
reference.

> **Note added March 2025:** clause 2.2 reflects past practice and is *not*
> current authority. The Pricing and Discounting Policy (DOC-PRC-001) sets a
> hard ceiling of 25 percent. Any deal requiring more must be restructured.
> Clause 2.2 is retained only to explain historical contracts.

## 3. Ramp deals

3.1 A ramp deal starts at a reduced seat count and steps up on agreed dates.
Ramps are the preferred alternative to a deep discount.

3.2 Commission on a ramp deal is calculated on the fully-ramped annual value,
not the starting value.

## 4. Renewals

4.1 Renewal commission is 3 percent of contract value.

4.2 Uplift achieved at renewal above 7 percent earns an additional 2 percent on
the incremental amount.
