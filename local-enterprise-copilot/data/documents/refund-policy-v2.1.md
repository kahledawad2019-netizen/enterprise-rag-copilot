# Refund and Credit Policy

**Northwind Cloud** · Version 2.1 · Effective 1 January 2025

This policy supersedes version 1.0 (DOC-REF-000) in full. Where a contract
signed before 1 January 2025 references the previous policy, the terms of the
signed contract prevail until renewal.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers monetary refunds and service credits for all Northwind Cloud
subscription products. It does not cover professional-services engagements,
which are governed by the individual statement of work.

## 2. Monthly plans

2.1 Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time. Cancellation takes
effect at the end of the current billing period.

2.2 No refund is issued for the remainder of a monthly billing period that has
already started. The service remains available until the period ends.

## 3. Annual plans

3.1 **Enterprise annual plans may be refunded on a pro-rata basis within the
first 30 days of the contract term.** The refund covers the unused portion of
the term, calculated to the day.

3.2 After day 30, annual contracts are not refundable. Customers may cancel
auto-renewal at any time before the renewal date.

3.3 Where a customer downgrades mid-term, the difference is issued as an
account credit rather than a cash refund. Credits expire 12 months after issue.

3.4 Professional and Starter annual plans follow the same 30-day rule, except
that the refund is capped at 50 percent of the annual value.

## 4. Service credits for SLA breaches

4.1 Service credits arising from an SLA breach are governed by the Service
Level Agreement (DOC-SLA-001), not by this policy.

4.2 Service credits are applied to the next invoice. They are never paid out
in cash, and they do not count towards the refund-rate metric.

## 5. Billing errors

5.1 A confirmed billing error is refunded in full regardless of plan type or
elapsed time, within 10 business days of confirmation.

5.2 Duplicate charges are refunded automatically once detected, without
requiring a customer request.

## 6. Approval thresholds

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**Can an enterprise customer get a refund after 6 months?**
No. Beyond day 30 of the annual term, no refund is available. The customer may
disable auto-renewal so that the contract ends at the term date.

**Does a partial refund affect the SLA?**
No. Service levels continue to apply for the remainder of the paid term.

**Are refunds issued in the original currency?**
Yes. Refunds are issued in the currency of the original invoice, at the
original exchange rate. Northwind Cloud does not absorb currency movement.
